# PiPal Curriculum Levels

This curriculum structure supports a progressive STEM journey for student learners.

## Beginner
- Devices are chosen for simple cause-and-effect experimentation.
- Prompts focus on playful interaction, sound, light, and immediate feedback.
- Students can explore concepts without prior programming experience.

## Intermediate
- Devices introduce sensing and measurement for real-world investigation.
- Prompts encourage combining multiple sensors and solving environmental problems.
- Lessons steer students toward experimentation, data, and safety thinking.

## Advanced
- Devices support imaging, navigation, radio exploration, and motion.
- Prompts promote systems thinking and invention rather than single-step tasks.
- Students are encouraged to build integrated projects with multiple advanced components.

## Design Notes
- Each level unlocks richer devices and more sophisticated project ideas.
- Prompts are framed as challenges to spark curiosity and creativity.
- The curriculum files are intentionally editable so instructors can update devices or prompts without changing application code.
